  QUICK MONGODB ATLAS SETUP (Copy these steps)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STEP 1: Go to https://www.mongodb.com/cloud/atlas/register
        → Sign up (use Google/GitHub for faster signup)

STEP 2: Click "Build a Database" → Choose "M0 FREE" → Click "Create"
        → Wait 2-3 minutes for cluster to be created

STEP 3: Click "Database Access" (left menu)
        → Click "Add New Database User"
        → Username: attendance_user
        → Password: (create and SAVE this password!)
        → Click "Add User"

STEP 4: Click "Network Access" (left menu)
        → Click "Add IP Address"
        → Click "Allow Access from Anywhere"
        → Click "Confirm"

STEP 5: Click "Database" (left menu)
        → Click "Connect" button on your cluster
        → Choose "Connect your application"
        → Copy the connection string

STEP 6: Open backend/.env file and replace MONGODB_URI with:
        mongodb+srv://attendance_user:YOUR_PASSWORD@cluster0.xxxxx.mongodb.net/attendance_system?retryWrites=true&w=majority
        
        (Replace YOUR_PASSWORD and cluster0.xxxxx with your actual values)

STEP 7: Restart backend server (Ctrl+C, then npm run dev)
        → Should see: ✅ Connected to MongoDB

STEP 8: Try registration at http://localhost:3000
